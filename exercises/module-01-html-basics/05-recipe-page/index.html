Classic Homemade Pancakes

A simple and delicious recipe for fluffy golden pancakes perfect for weekend mornings.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 3 1/2 teaspoons baking powder
  • 1 tablespoon white sugar
  • 1 1/4 cups milk
  • 1 egg
  • 3 tablespoons melted butter

Instructions

  1. Sift the flour, baking powder, and sugar together in a large bowl.
  2. Pour in the milk, egg, and melted butter; mix until smooth.
  3. Heat a lightly oiled griddle or frying pan over medium-high heat.
  4. Pour batter onto the griddle and brown on both sides, serving hot.
