Dr. Norman Borlaug

The man who saved a billion lives

Here's a timeline of Dr. Borlaug's life:

  • 1914 - Born in Cresco, Iowa
  • 1933 - Leaves his family's farm to attend the University of Minnesota, studying forestry
  • 1941 - Recruited by DuPont to work on agricultural chemicals and bactericides
  • 1944 - Moves to Mexico to lead the International Maize and Wheat Improvement Center
  • 1953 - Successfully crosses high-yield dwarf wheat varieties resistant to rust fungus
  • 1966 - Introduces high-yield wheat strains to India and Pakistan, averting massive famine
  • 1970 - Awarded the Nobel Peace Prize for his monumental contributions to global food security
  • 2009 - Dies at the age of 95, leaving a legacy of science serving humanity

"Borlaug's life and achievement are testimony to the far-reaching contribution that one man's towering intellect, persistence and scientific vision can make to human peace and progress."

-- Indian Prime Minister Manmohan Singh
